Common Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We strive to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days (Monday-Friday). For urgent matters, please call our support line for immediate assistance.

Do you offer emergency mental health services?

While we provide mental health resources and support, we are not an emergency service. If you're in crisis, please contact your local emergency services or crisis hotline immediately.

Can I schedule a one-on-one consultation?

Yes, we offer consultations with our mental health professionals. Please contact us to schedule an appointment based on availability.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

You can manage your subscription through your account settings on our website or app. If you need assistance, our support team is happy to help.
