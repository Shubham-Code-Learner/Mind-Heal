Frequently Asked Questions

How much sleep do I need?

Most adults need 7-9 hours of sleep per night, but individual needs may vary. Use our sleep calculator to find your optimal sleep duration.

What is sleep hygiene?

Sleep hygiene refers to habits and practices that promote good sleep quality. This includes maintaining a consistent sleep schedule, creating a comfortable sleep environment, and avoiding stimulants before bedtime.

How can I improve my sleep quality?

Improving sleep quality involves multiple factors: maintaining a regular sleep schedule, creating a comfortable sleep environment, managing stress, and adopting healthy lifestyle habits.

What are sleep cycles?

Sleep cycles are 90-minute periods during which we progress through different sleep stages. A complete night's sleep typically includes 4-6 sleep cycles.
