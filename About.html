Our Journey to Better Mental Health

Mind Heal was founded on the belief that everyone deserves access to quality mental health support. Discover our story, mission, and the passionate team behind our platform.

Our Mission & Vision

At Mind Heal, we're committed to breaking down barriers to mental health care through innovative technology and evidence-based practices. Our mission is to make mental wellness accessible, affordable, and effective for everyone.

We envision a world where mental health is prioritized as much as physical health, where stigma is eliminated, and where people have the tools they need to thrive emotionally and psychologically.

Founded in 2018 by a team of psychologists and technologists, Mind Heal has grown from a small startup to a leading mental wellness platform serving over 1 million users worldwide.

Our Story

2015

The Beginning

Our founder, Dr. Sarah Johnson, recognized the growing need for accessible mental health support while working in community clinics.

2017

Research & Development

Two years of research and prototype development with psychologists, therapists, and technology experts.

2018

Official Launch

Mind Heal launched with basic meditation guides and mood tracking features, serving 5,000 users in the first month.

2020

Pandemic Response

During COVID-19, we offered free services to frontline workers and expanded our team to meet growing demand.

2022

AI Integration

Launched our AI mental health assistant, trained by clinical psychologists to provide immediate support.

2023

Global Expansion

Expanded services to 15 new countries and localized content in 8 languages.
